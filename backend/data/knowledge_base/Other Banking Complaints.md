# Other Banking Complaints — Banking SOP

**Category:** Other banking
**Covers:** Complaints that do not fit Cards, Accounts, Loans, or Collections & Credit reporting — e.g. fee disputes, account opening/closing issues, general customer service escalations, virtual currency, financial service general complaints

---

## Unexpected or Hidden Fees

**Complaint type:** Customer was charged a fee they did not expect or were not told about.

**Common Causes:**
- Monthly maintenance fee triggered when minimum balance was not met
- Paper statement fee added without notice
- Wire transfer fee not disclosed at initiation
- Foreign transaction fee on international purchase

**Responsible Department:** Deposit Operations / Customer Service

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Identify the fee code and pull the fee schedule on file for the account type.
2. Check whether the fee was disclosed in the account agreement and/or fee schedule the customer received at opening.
3. If the fee was disclosed: explain clearly when and why it applied; point to the specific account agreement section.
4. If the fee was NOT disclosed (Regulation DD violation): refund the fee; escalate to Compliance.
5. If the customer was not notified of a fee change at least 30 days in advance (as required by Regulation DD for deposit accounts): refund the fee and initiate a compliance review.
6. Offer to convert the account to a no-fee product if available and appropriate.

## Account Closure — Customer Disputes or Requests Reason

**Complaint type:** Bank closed the customer's account without adequate notice, or the customer believes the closure was in error.

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Pull the account closure reason code.
2. Confirm whether the 30-day notice requirement was met (or if an immediate closure was legally permitted — e.g. suspected fraud, legal order).
3. If notice was not given: issue an apology letter; discuss whether reinstatement is possible.
4. If the closure was for suspected fraud or AML reasons: do not disclose the specific internal reason; advise the customer they may contact their local branch for further discussion.
5. Confirm that all remaining funds are returned via check within 5 business days.

## Account Opening — Application Denied

**Complaint type:** Customer was denied a checking or savings account and wants to know why.

**Legal Framework:** Adverse Action Notice under Regulation B (Equal Credit Opportunity Act — applicable to accounts considered "credit" or where a consumer report was used).

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Confirm whether a consumer reporting agency (ChexSystems, Early Warning Services) report was used.
2. If so: the customer is entitled to a free copy of the report; provide the name and contact of the CRA.
3. If the denial was due to a negative ChexSystems record: explain the reason codes; advise the customer to dispute any inaccurate information directly with ChexSystems (similar to a credit bureau dispute process).
4. If the denial was due to an OFAC match (sanctions list): do not disclose the specific reason — follow the institution's OFAC escalation procedure.
5. Suggest a "second-chance" account product if available.

## Virtual Currency / Crypto-Related Service Complaint

**Complaint type:** Customer cannot access a virtual currency wallet linked to a bank account, or a transfer to/from a crypto exchange was blocked.

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Clarify the specific issue: bank-side ACH block, crypto exchange issue, or wallet access.
2. If the bank blocked an ACH to a crypto exchange: review the institution's crypto-transfer policy; explain the reason (fraud prevention, regulatory compliance).
3. If the transaction was fraudulent: follow standard ACH fraud protocol.
4. If the customer was scammed into sending funds to a crypto wallet: advise that these transactions are generally irreversible; assist with filing a police report and an FTC complaint at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
5. Do not advise on cryptocurrency investments or tax implications — refer to a financial advisor.

## General Escalation — Customer Requesting a Supervisor

**Complaint type:** Customer is dissatisfied with prior resolution and demands to speak with a manager.

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Acknowledge the customer's frustration without agreeing to any specific outcome.
2. Summarize the prior interaction and what the bank has done so far.
3. Transfer to a supervisor or schedule a callback within 4 business hours.
4. The supervisor should review the full contact history before engaging the customer.
5. If the issue remains unresolved after escalation: offer to open a formal written complaint; provide the CFPB complaint portal address (consumerfinance.gov/complaint).
6. Document the escalation reason and outcome in the case management system.

## Customer Requests Record of All Complaints Filed

**Complaint type:** Customer wants a copy of prior complaints or requests confirmation a complaint was filed.

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Provide the case/reference numbers for all complaints filed in the past 12 months.
2. The bank is not required to share internal investigation notes, but must confirm a complaint was received and is being addressed.
3. Advise the customer they may also check complaint status directly with the CFPB if they filed there.
