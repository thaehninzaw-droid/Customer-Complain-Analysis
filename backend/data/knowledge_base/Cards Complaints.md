# Cards Complaints — Banking SOP

**Category:** Cards
**Covers:** Credit cards, debit cards, prepaid cards, chargebacks, unauthorized transactions

---

## Unauthorized or Fraudulent Charge

**Complaint type:** Customer reports a charge they do not recognize or did not authorize.

**Common Causes:**
- Card number stolen or skimmed at a point-of-sale terminal
- Online purchase fraud or phishing leading to card compromise
- Family member or authorized user made a purchase without explicit consent
- Merchant processed a duplicate or incorrect charge

**Responsible Department:** Fraud / Cards Operations

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Verify customer identity with full name, last 4 digits of card, and billing address.
2. Ask for the date, merchant name, and amount of the disputed transaction.
3. Place a temporary hold on the card if active fraud is suspected.
4. File a fraud dispute / chargeback in the card system and provide the customer a reference number.
5. Issue a provisional credit within 1–5 business days per Regulation Z / EFTA timelines.
6. Order a replacement card with a new number; confirm mailing address.
7. Advise customer to monitor for additional unauthorized activity.
8. Escalate to the Fraud Investigations team if the total exceeds $500 or if multiple cards are involved.

**Follow-up:** Check dispute resolution within 10 business days and notify customer of outcome.

## Billing Error on Credit Card Statement

**Complaint type:** Customer says a charge is wrong — wrong amount, wrong date, or duplicate billing.

**Common Causes:**
- Merchant submitted an incorrect transaction amount
- Subscription renewed unexpectedly after cancellation
- Promotional rate expired; customer charged standard rate without notice

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Pull up the transaction history for the card.
2. Ask for documentation (receipt, email confirmation) if the customer has it.
3. Contact the merchant directly if the error is clearly a merchant mistake.
4. Process a billing dispute under the Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA).
5. Provide customer a written confirmation of dispute initiation.
6. Resolve within 2 billing cycles per FCBA requirements.

## Card Declined Despite Available Balance

**Complaint type:** Card transaction declined; customer believes they have sufficient funds.

**Common Causes:**
- Spending or daily transaction limit reached
- Card flagged by fraud detection system for unusual activity
- Merchant category blocked (e.g. international, gambling)
- Card expired or deactivated

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Check the card status — active, blocked, or expired.
2. Review recent fraud flags; clear if triggered in error.
3. Review applicable spending limits and advise customer if a temporary increase is possible.
4. If the card is expired, issue a replacement.
5. Advise customer to call the number on the back of the card for immediate transactions.

---

## Prepaid Card — Funds Not Loaded or Missing

**Complaint type:** Customer says funds added to a prepaid card have not appeared, or a balance disappeared.

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Confirm the load method (direct deposit, retailer reload, bank transfer) and reference number.
2. Check the reload history in the prepaid card platform.
3. If the load failed, initiate a trace with the load network.
4. If funds show loaded but balance is zero, escalate to Prepaid Operations for investigation.
5. Provide customer a written update within 2 business days.

---

## Credit Limit Reduction or Account Closure

**Complaint type:** Customer received no notice, or believes the reduction/closure was unfair.

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Pull the adverse action notice on file; confirm it was sent within 30 days (ECOA/Regulation B).
2. Explain the reason codes in plain language.
3. If the customer disputes the underlying data, direct them to file a credit bureau dispute.
4. If hardship is claimed, transfer to Credit Counseling / Hardship team for review.
5. Document all communications.
