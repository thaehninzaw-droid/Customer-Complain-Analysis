# Accounts Complaints — Banking SOP

**Category:** Accounts
**Covers:** Checking accounts, savings accounts, money transfers, wire transfers, overdrafts, account holds

---

## Unauthorized Withdrawal or Transfer

**Complaint type:** Customer did not authorize a withdrawal or transfer from their account.

**Common Causes:**
- Online banking credentials compromised (phishing, malware)
- ACH debit initiated by a third party without valid authorization
- Zelle / P2P transfer scam — customer was tricked into sending funds
- Shared account — dispute between authorized users

**Responsible Department:** Fraud / Deposit Operations

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Verify the customer's identity (security questions, PIN, ID verification).
2. Ask for the date, amount, and destination of the unauthorized transfer.
3. Place an immediate hold on additional outgoing transfers if the account is actively being drained.
4. File an ACH dispute or wire recall if applicable.
5. If Zelle scam: advise that Zelle transactions are bank-to-bank — initiate a recall request immediately; outcomes are not guaranteed.
6. Provide a provisional credit within 10 business days per Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFTA).
7. Change account credentials (reset online banking password, issue new debit card).
8. Escalate to fraud team for amounts over $1,000 or recurring incidents.

## Overdraft Fees — Dispute or Waiver Request

**Complaint type:** Customer disputes an overdraft fee or requests a courtesy waiver.

**Common Causes:**
- Transaction ordering (highest to lowest) led to more overdrafts than expected
- Customer believes an incoming deposit should have cleared first
- Enrolled in overdraft protection but fees still charged

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Pull the transaction chronology for the day in question.
2. Explain the bank's transaction ordering policy clearly.
3. Check how many overdraft fee waivers the customer has received in the past 12 months.
4. Offer a one-time courtesy reversal if within policy (typically 1–2 reversals per year for customers in good standing).
5. If the customer consistently overdrafts, offer to enroll them in overdraft protection or a linked savings account.
6. Document the waiver in the customer notes.

## Account Frozen or Funds on Hold

**Complaint type:** Customer cannot access their funds; account is frozen or a hold was placed.

**Common Causes:**
- Suspected fraud or suspicious activity detected
- Large check deposit under standard hold policy (Regulation CC)
- Legal hold (garnishment, levy)
- Inactive account reactivation

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Identify the reason for the hold in the account system.
2. If Regulation CC hold: explain the hold schedule and when funds will be available (typically 2–7 business days for large checks). Provide the written hold notice if not already sent.
3. If legal hold (garnishment): advise customer to consult legal counsel; the bank cannot unilaterally lift court-ordered holds.
4. If fraud flag: escalate to Fraud for a same-day review if customer has urgent financial need.
5. If suspected identity theft locked the account, initiate identity verification process.

## Money Transfer — Funds Not Received

**Complaint type:** Customer sent money via wire or ACH but recipient has not received it.

**Common Causes:**
- Incorrect routing or account number provided
- Transfer initiated after cutoff time; processed next business day
- Correspondent bank delay (international wire)
- Compliance review triggered (AML hold)

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Confirm the transfer details: date, amount, routing number, account number, reference/confirmation number.
2. Check the transfer status in the wire/ACH system.
3. If incorrect account number was provided: initiate an ACH return or wire recall immediately; fees may apply.
4. For domestic ACH: resolution typically within 2–5 business days.
5. For international wire: initiate a tracer with the correspondent bank; advise 5–15 business days.
6. If compliance hold: do not disclose specific AML reasons; advise the customer that the bank will contact them if documentation is needed.

## ATM Swallowed Card or Dispensed Wrong Amount

**Complaint type:** ATM retained the customer's card, or dispensed less cash than requested.

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Ask for the ATM location, date, time, and transaction reference number if displayed.
2. For a swallowed card: immediately block the card to prevent misuse; order a replacement.
3. For a cash discrepancy: log an ATM dispute; the machine will be balanced at end of day and shortfalls reconciled within 10 business days.
4. Provide provisional credit for the undispensed amount while investigation is pending.
5. Update the customer when the ATM reconciliation is complete.
