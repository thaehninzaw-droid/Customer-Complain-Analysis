# General / Other Complaints - Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope
Anything that doesn't fit Billing, Financial, Technical, or Service -
general plan questions, cancellation requests, account/address
changes, and miscellaneous inquiries.

## Step 1 - Clarify intent
"Others" is a catch-all, so the first job is figuring out whether this
is genuinely miscellaneous or actually belongs to one of the other four
categories once you read past the surface wording (e.g. a "general
question about my plan" that turns out to really be about a specific
charge is Billing, not Others). Re-tag the ticket's category if so -
don't force a fix into the wrong playbook.

## Step 2 - Handle common cases
- **Plan upgrade/downgrade questions**: explain available tiers and
  pricing; process the change same-day if the customer confirms.
- **Cancellation requests**: do NOT use retention scripts that make it
  hard to actually cancel - confirm the request, explain the
  effective date and any final bill, and process it. Excessive
  friction here is itself a Service-quality risk.
- **Address/account changes**: verify identity per standard KYC steps,
  then process. If a website bug is blocking self-service (a known
  issue as of this writing), process manually and flag the bug to
  engineering rather than telling the customer to "try again later."

## Step 3 - Escalation criteria
Escalate if the request involves legal/compliance matters (e.g. a
subpoena, law-enforcement request, or an accessibility accommodation
request) - these should never be handled ad hoc by a general agent.

## Response time target
First response within 8 business hours; most requests in this category
resolve same-day once clarified.
