# Billing Complaints - Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope
Covers complaints about incorrect charges, double billing, unexplained
fee increases, late fees disputed by the customer, and confusion about
a bill's line items.

## Step 1 - Verify the charge
Pull up the customer's last 3 billing cycles. Compare the disputed
line item against their plan's rate card. Common root causes:
- A promotional rate expired and the account reverted to standard
  pricing without the customer being notified in advance.
- A one-time equipment or installation fee was billed in a later cycle
  than expected.
- The customer was double-billed due to a payment processing retry
  (check the payment gateway log, not just the invoice).

## Step 2 - Decide the outcome
- If the charge was a genuine billing error: issue a credit to the
  account within 1 business day. Do not require the customer to call
  back to confirm - proactively email a summary of the correction.
- If the charge is correct but the customer wasn't clearly informed
  (e.g. a promo-expiry): still consider a one-time courtesy credit for
  first-time disputes, especially for accounts older than 12 months.
- If the charge is correct and previously disclosed: explain the
  charge clearly, point to the specific plan terms, and close the
  ticket as resolved even if the customer remains unhappy with the
  policy itself.

## Step 3 - Escalation criteria
Escalate to a billing supervisor if:
- The disputed amount exceeds $200.
- The customer has filed 2+ billing complaints in the last 90 days.
- The customer explicitly requests a supervisor or threatens to cancel.

## Response time target
First response within 4 business hours; resolution within 2 business
days for straightforward corrections.
