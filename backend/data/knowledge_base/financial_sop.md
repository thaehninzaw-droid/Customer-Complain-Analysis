# Financial Complaints - Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope
Covers refund requests, deposit returns, promotional credits that
were never applied, and payment-plan disputes. Distinct from Billing:
Financial complaints are about money owed BACK to the customer, not
about a charge being wrong.

## Step 1 - Confirm eligibility
- Refunds: check the cancellation date against the refund policy
  window (typically 30 days from service end for unused prepaid
  balance).
- Deposits: security deposits are refundable once the account has
  been closed for 30 days with no outstanding balance and no unreturned
  equipment.
- Promotional credits: check the original promo terms - most require
  the account to stay active for a minimum term (commonly 3 months)
  before the credit posts.

## Step 2 - Process or explain
- If eligible: initiate the refund/credit immediately. Refunds to the
  original payment method typically take 5-7 business days to appear;
  tell the customer that explicit window rather than "soon."
- If not yet eligible (e.g. promo term not yet met): explain the exact
  date it will post, and set a reminder so it isn't missed.
- If the customer has already been waiting longer than the stated
  processing window: treat this as a service failure, not just a
  financial one - apologize for the delay specifically, not just the
  underlying issue, and process manually rather than waiting on the
  automated batch.

## Step 3 - Escalation criteria
Escalate to Finance if:
- The refund/credit amount exceeds $150.
- The automated refund has failed twice already.
- The customer disputes the deposit deduction amount itself (not just
  the timing).

## Response time target
First response within 4 business hours; funds should move within 5-7
business days of approval, or same-day for amounts under $50 with
manager sign-off.
