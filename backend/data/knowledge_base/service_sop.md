# Service Complaints - Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope
Covers complaints about representative conduct (rudeness, unhelpfulness),
long hold times, being transferred repeatedly without resolution, and
promised callbacks that never happened.

## Step 1 - Acknowledge directly
Service complaints are about the EXPERIENCE, not just the underlying
issue - always acknowledge the experience explicitly before jumping to
fix the original problem ("I'm sorry that call didn't go the way it
should have - let's get this sorted now"). Do not get defensive about
a colleague's conduct; investigate separately, not in front of the
customer.

## Step 2 - Resolve the underlying issue AND the experience gap
1. Identify what the customer was originally trying to accomplish (the
   root complaint may be logged under Service but the actual fix might
   be Billing/Financial/Technical) - resolve that first.
2. If a callback was promised and missed: call back immediately,
   don't wait for the customer to follow up again.
3. If the customer was transferred multiple times: this call/chat
   should be the last transfer - if it needs to go elsewhere, the
   current agent stays on the line/thread to make the handoff rather
   than transferring blind.

## Step 3 - Conduct follow-up
- Any complaint naming a specific representative's conduct gets
  logged for a supervisor review, separate from the customer-facing
  resolution - this is an internal process, don't mention it to the
  customer as if it's a punishment.
- Offer a goodwill gesture (account credit or fee waiver) for
  confirmed multi-transfer or missed-callback cases, even if the
  underlying issue was resolved correctly.

## Step 4 - Escalation criteria
Escalate to a supervisor immediately (during the interaction, not
after) if:
- The customer explicitly asks for a supervisor.
- This is a repeat Service complaint from the same customer.
- The customer describes conduct that could indicate a policy
  violation (discrimination, harassment, etc.) - escalate to HR/
  compliance per internal policy, not just a service supervisor.

## Response time target
First response within 2 business hours; any promised callback happens
within the promised window, no exceptions.
