# **Common Causes:** 

- The transaction may be under financial review or verification 

- Account or transaction information may not match the financial record 

- A third-party financial institution may have delayed or rejected the transaction 

- A fraud-prevention or compliance check may have placed the transaction on hold 

# **Responsible Department: Financial** 

# **Resolution Steps:** 

1. Verify the customer identity and affected financial account 

2. Collect the transaction date, amount, and available reference details 

3. Review the transaction history and current processing status 

4. Check for rejection, reversal, hold, or verification indicators 

5. Correct eligible account or transaction records 

6. Escalate unresolved financial discrepancies to the Finance team 

7. Confirm the outcome and any required next action with the customer
